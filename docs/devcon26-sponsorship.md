# DevCon26 sponsorship

## 2026-10-10: Minimal package comparison

The event's sponsors section contains six compact comparison packages:
Community Contributor (GHS 5,000 / 2 passes), Community Collaborator
(GHS 10,000 / 4), Community Builder (GHS 20,000 / 6), Community Catalyst
(GHS 30,000 / 8), Community Champion (GHS 50,000 / 10), and Title Partner
(GHS 70,000+ / bespoke). Prices and pass allocations are unchanged. These are
concise package summaries; the original PDF is the reference for full benefits
and terms.

The overview uses three columns and two rows on desktop, two columns on tablet,
and a compact single-column stack on mobile. Uniform ivory surfaces, compact
yellow family tags, standalone display-font tier names, and prominent prices make
the comparison clear. Community and Title appear as rectangular tags inside
their headings; the full package name remains visible and accessible without
duplicating any text. Pink benefit checks and ivory outlined enquiry buttons
bring the existing brand accents into the cards. Decorative geometric
illustrations, tinted headers, and pass-count stamps stay removed. The GHS
prefix is visually smaller than the amount; each exact price and plain-text
pass allocation remains visible and accessible. Title Partner passes are
tailored to the partnership, not given a fabricated count.

Each package shows its purpose, three concise highlights, pass allocation, and
key conditions. Package-detail accordions and their full-benefit source data
are removed; interested visitors can download the PDF beside the heading.
Individual package photographs and alternating layout rules remain removed.
No package is marked as recommended or most popular.

The PDF download remains beside the heading, and the custom-partnership closing
row belongs to the same overview. Enquiry buttons reveal a quiet yellow tint
on hover, keyboard focus, and press using a short opacity transition behind
the label. Arrows use transform-only feedback, with hover limited to fine
pointers and movement removed from press/hover feedback for reduced-motion
settings. There is no repeated or decorative card motion. Keyboard focus
remains visible, including forced-colors mode. CSS subgrid aligns the six
comparison fields within each row when supported, with an ordinary grid
layout as the fallback.

The original supplied sponsorship PDF is served unchanged at
`/downloads/devcon26-sponsorship-packages.pdf` as a download, not an embed.
The general pitch deck is not published. The overview retains the approved
website venue and month instead of importing the PDF's exact date, forecast,
or tentative keynote. Speaking and programme opportunities remain subject to
DevCongress approval. Champion exclusivity is limited to the agreed category
at the same positioning level; Title Partner terms are agreed individually.

All sponsorship enquiry links open `mailto:events@devcongress.org` with a
DevCon26 subject. Package buttons say "Choose this package" and also prefill
a short email body identifying the selected package and its exact price, with
editable organisation/contact-name placeholders and a request for the next
steps to confirm sponsorship. The visitor reviews and sends the email in their
own email app. No form submits, email sends, or sponsorship reservations happen
on the website.
The shared footer's general contact remains unchanged.

The partners collection is fetched once on the DevCon26 page. Only Kweku Tech
Media is selected as a current sponsor. After FAQs, the final main section lists
all six historical partners alphabetically, including Kweku Tech Media. Shared
partner records and the homepage remain unchanged.

## 2026-10-10: Download interaction feedback

The sponsorship PDF card highlights its ivory surface and quiet edge on hover,
keyboard focus, and press. Fine-pointer hover adds a 1px lift and a small pink
arrow movement; keyboard focus retains the visible outline without lifting the
card. A short press response also works on touch devices. CSS transitions use
only transform and opacity, with movement removed for reduced-motion settings
and system colours used in forced-colors mode. The download destination, file
name, and original PDF remain unchanged.

## 2026-10-10: Focused sponsorship actions

The generic "Become a sponsor" button is removed from the introduction. The
package-selection buttons are the main sponsorship path; the shared PDF
download and custom-partnership action remain available. The vertical gap
between sponsor recognition and package comparison now uses the existing
32px spacing token across breakpoints, without changing the desktop columns.
Package email drafts, sponsor records, and the PDF remain unchanged.
